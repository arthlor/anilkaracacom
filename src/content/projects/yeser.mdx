# Yeser

Yeser was built as a calm, repeatable mobile experience. The goal was not to turn journaling into a complex productivity system, but to make reflection feel easier to return to.

## Product framing

The interface is intentionally lightweight. Streaks and prompts create structure, but the experience stays focused on reducing friction rather than increasing feature count.

## Why this case study matters

The app shows the difference between designing a pleasant concept and shipping a usable mobile product. It demonstrates execution across interaction flow, product pacing, and release discipline.
