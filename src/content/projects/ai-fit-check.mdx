# AI Fit Check

AI Fit Check was built as a consumer mobile product, not as an isolated AI demo. The goal was to make image uploads, response handling, and the feedback tone feel native to a lightweight mobile experience.

## Product framing

The app tests a simple thesis: users will engage more easily with AI if the interaction loop is short, opinionated, and clearly packaged. Instead of asking users to configure a system, the app centers a direct photo-to-feedback flow.

## Why it matters

For portfolio purposes, the case study proves product execution around AI features. It is about turning an AI-assisted capability into a shipped interface with mobile constraints, visual judgment, and release discipline.
