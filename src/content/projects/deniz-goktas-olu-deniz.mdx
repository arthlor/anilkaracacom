# Deniz Göktaş: Ölü Deniz (From Satire to Prosecution)

On July 3, 2026, Turkish stand-up comedian Deniz Göktaş was arrested in Istanbul following the online release of his show "Ölü Deniz". This case study deconstructs the legal arguments, maps the narrative structure of the show, and visualizes the statistical realities of the criminal charges he faces.

## Satire Under the Microscope

The investigation focuses on ten specific passages from Göktaş’s 90-minute performance, which prosecutors isolated from the broader context. The charges relate to:
- **TCK 216/3**: Insulting religious values
- **TCK 299**: Insulting the President

By analyzing the full 11,208-word transcript, this project shows that the questioned jokes represent only a small fraction of the performance, which primarily traverses personal history, class struggles, and media satire.

## The Evidentiary Weight of Statistics

Utilizing the official 2025 Ministry of Justice judicial statistics, this project contextualizes the TCK 216 and TCK 299/301 caseloads. The data reveals that the majority of these prosecutions do not end in final prison sentences, raising critical questions about the necessity and proportionality of pre-trial detention.
