# Deniz Göktaş: Ölü Deniz (Thematic & Structural Analysis)

Following the online release of Turkish stand-up comedian Deniz Göktaş's show "Ölü Deniz," this project maps the structural and narrative flow of his performance.

## Thematic Structure

The project organizes Göktaş’s performance to look at the broader context of his humor:
- **Thematic Balance**: The show is divided into 34 chronological sections covering diverse themes such as daily life, family, and media culture.
- **Contextual Reading**: By mapping the entire transcript, we see how specific jokes are situated within a larger comedic narrative rather than being isolated statements.
- **Narrative Flow**: The analysis highlights how the performance shifts between personal storytelling and social satire.

## Timeline of the Release

- **June 24**: The show "Ölü Deniz" was uploaded to YouTube, quickly reaching a wide audience.
- **Late June**: The show generated significant public discussion online, with individual clips shared widely.
- **July**: The project was compiled to document the complete structure of the show as it originally aired.

## Targets of Satire

The show mocks public figures and groups across the political and social spectrum:
- **Politicians**: Parodies both government and opposition figures, including Recep Tayyip Erdoğan, Ekrem İmamoğlu, Özgür Özel, and Ümit Özdağ.
- **Media & Academics**: Satirizes prominent commentators and scholars like Fatih Altaylı, Celal Şengör, and İlber Ortaylı.
- **Social Groups**: Pokes fun at the contradictions of both secular and conservative segments of Turkish society.
- **Self-Deprecation**: Focuses heavily on the comedian’s own background, family, and career struggles.
