# Op.Dr. Zeynep Esin Tuncay

This project is built around trust before polish. Patients arriving on the site need to understand the doctor&apos;s areas of expertise, feel oriented quickly, and see a clear path toward contact without getting lost inside generic clinic-site clutter.

## Service architecture

The homepage pushes the major service areas to the front. That matters because visitors are often arriving with a specific need, not with time to browse a vague brand story. The design therefore works as a sorting mechanism first, helping users move from uncertainty to the right treatment area with minimal friction.

## Conversion logic

The site keeps appointment intent visible through direct contact prompts, repeated calls to action, and a structure that avoids making booking feel like a buried secondary task. Blog content and Instagram embeds also broaden the trust surface, making the website feel active and current instead of frozen.

## Why this belongs in the portfolio

This case study shows a different kind of product judgment than a consumer app or editorial project. The value here is translating expertise into a web experience that feels approachable, credible, and useful for people making sensitive healthcare decisions.
