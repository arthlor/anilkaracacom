# ChoreUs

ChoreUs reframes a familiar problem: how to make shared household work feel structured without making the experience feel bureaucratic.

## Product thesis

The app uses gamified patterns because the problem is behavioral as much as organizational. Shared chores often fail not because people cannot list them, but because there is no satisfying rhythm for completing them together.

## Why this belongs in the portfolio

This project shows product engineering in a consumer setting. The interesting part is not just the codebase; it is the translation of motivation and accountability into an interface that is simple enough to use repeatedly.
