# Bohça

Bohça is a Turkish-first planning app for çeyiz preparation. It treats the work as more than a checklist: users need to organize rooms and categories, attach product context, watch spending, track gifts, and coordinate with the person they are building the list with.

## Product framing

The app is built around a shared preparation space. Instead of keeping separate notes or spreadsheets, a couple can work inside one structured Bohça, with the core list, item details, budgets, gifts, moving boxes, and reminders connected through the same product model.

## Why this belongs in the portfolio

Bohça shows mobile product ownership at the level that matters commercially: a culturally specific problem, real App Store delivery, account and workspace state, backend data contracts, subscription packaging, and Turkish UX copy that has to feel natural rather than translated.
