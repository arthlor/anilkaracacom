Izmir's hometown-origin data shows where shared-origin networks become visible at district scale. Start by selecting a district, then compare its strongest external-origin groups with the citywide pattern.

<IzmirHometownsExplorer client:load />

## What to notice

- Izmir-born residents dominate citywide totals, so the useful comparison is the non-Izmir origin mix.
- Some districts show balanced external-origin profiles, while others are shaped by stronger hometown clusters.
- The map is orientation; the ranking and citywide comparison carry the main read.

## Data note

The local module contains 30 district profiles, 300 ranked district-origin entries, and 80 citywide origin totals. The explorer should be read as a pattern-finding view over those local public-data extracts, not as a complete population accounting table.
