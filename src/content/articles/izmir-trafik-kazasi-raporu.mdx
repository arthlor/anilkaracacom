## What the records show

Three findings hold across the views:

1. **A small group of streets repeatedly carries the highest counts.** Yeşildere, Anadolu, Mürselpaşa, and Akçay remain near the top across multiple years.

2. **Breakdowns and property-damage collisions make up much of the volume.** Serious crashes matter most for safety, but routine incidents shape day-to-day disruption.

3. **Timing matters.** Weekday commute periods—and Friday evening in particular—deserve closer operational attention.
